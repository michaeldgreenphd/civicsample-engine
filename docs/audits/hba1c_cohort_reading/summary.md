## Ambiguities in the Assignment and Literal Choices Applied
Where the assignment specification met structural features of ClinicalTrials.gov API v2 that admitted more than one literal interpretation, the most literal non-inferential rule was applied uniformly across all 248 records:

1. **Posted primary HbA1c measures with zero analysed participants / empty `classes` (early-terminated trials)**: When an entry in `resultsSection.outcomeMeasuresModule.outcomeMeasures` has `type == "PRIMARY"` and is about HbA1c, `hba1c_primary_measure_posted` is coded as `yes` even if `denoms` is `0` and `classes`/`analyses` are absent (because the primary outcome measure object is posted in `outcomeMeasuresModule`), with unpopulated fields (`unit_as_posted`, `parameter_type`, `dispersion_type`, etc.) recorded literally as `not_stated`.
2. **Multi-row (`classes`) HbA1c primary outcome measures and co-reported estimands**: Some posted primary Change-from-Baseline HbA1c measures include helper rows in `classes` for Baseline HbA1c and/or endpoint Week HbA1c alongside the Change row, or present only Baseline and endpoint rows in `classes` while stating in `title`/`description` that the endpoint is change from baseline; all `arm_measurements` rows are preserved verbatim in `cohort_sheet.csv` and `readings.jsonl`, while `construct` codes the measure's stated primary construct. In addition, when a single `outcomeMeasures[i]` co-reports two observation-period estimands with opposite post-rescue rules (for example `In-trial` including post-rescue values and `On-treatment without rescue medication` excluding post-rescue values), `data_after_rescue_therapy` codes the first-listed observation period (`classes[0]`) and flags `both_rescue_estimands_present = yes` (observed in 10/248 trials with a posted HbA1c primary measure: NCT02607865, NCT02715258, NCT02827708, NCT02863328, NCT02863419, NCT02906930, NCT02964247, NCT03018028, NCT03021187, NCT04017832).
3. **Time frames stated in months or days rather than weeks**: Under the non-conversion rule (*"Do not infer, harmonise, convert, impute or correct anything"*), `time_point_weeks` is given a numeric value only when `timeFrame` states one fixed post-baseline time point in **weeks** (including baseline-to-week expressions such as `"Baseline to week 26"` or `"Baseline and Week 24"`). Time frames stated solely in months or days (such as `"Month 6"`, `"6 months"`, or `"Day 168"`) without stating a week number in `timeFrame`, as well as open ranges (`"Up to 24 weeks"`) or multiple post-baseline time points, are coded as `not_one_fixed_time_point`.
4. **`unit_class` on responder/proportion measures**: `unit_class` is classified strictly from `outcomeMeasures[i].unitOfMeasure`. On responder endpoints (`proportion_reaching_hba1c_target` or composite endpoints), a posted unit such as `"Percentage of participants"`, `"% of subjects"`, or `"Participants"` denotes percentage or count of *participants* rather than `% HbA1c`, and is therefore coded as `other` (never `percent_hba1c`).
5. **`population_class` when the record uses `ITT` / `Intent-to-Treat` vs `Full Analysis Set`**: Following *"Use the record's own term to choose; if it uses a term without defining it, choose the class for that term and set population_defined = no"*, records naming `Full Analysis Set` / `FAS` are coded `full_analysis_set` (`population_defined = yes` if defined, `no` if undefined). Because the 8 specified `population_class` values do not have a separate `intention_to_treat` label, an undefined `ITT` / `Intent-to-Treat` label maps to `all_randomised` (`population_defined = no`), whereas an `ITT` or `mITT` label accompanied by a stated restriction maps to `all_randomised` (randomised only), `all_randomised_and_treated` (randomised and >=1 dose only), or `modified_intention_to_treat_with_stated_condition` (randomised plus baseline/post-baseline measurement or other stated condition, with `population_defined = yes`).
6. **`comparator_class` in multi-dose trials**: Determined strictly from the measure's arm descriptions (`outcomeMeasures[i].groups`, or `protocolSection.armsInterventionsModule.armGroups` only if measure group descriptions are absent): one or more doses of an investigational drug/regimen vs placebo only (with or without identical continuing background therapy) is coded `placebo`; one or more doses of an investigational drug/regimen vs a distinct active drug only is coded `active_drug`; multiple doses/regimens/formulations of the same drug with neither placebo nor a distinct active comparator drug is coded `dose_comparison_of_same_drug`; presence of both a placebo control arm and a distinct active comparator drug arm in the same measure is coded `mixed`; single-arm measures or insufficient arm text are coded `not_determinable`.
7. **Legacy ClinicalTrials.gov `nonInferiorityType` buckets and single-ID `groupIds` in `analyses[]`**: ClinicalTrials.gov API v2 stores legacy PRS selections as combined enums (`SUPERIORITY_OR_OTHER`, `NON_INFERIORITY_OR_EQUIVALENCE`). When neither the enum nor any prose in the measure/analysis (`nonInferiorityComment`, `pValueComment`, `groupDescription`, `statisticalComment`, `estimateComment`, `description`) disambiguates between the two halves of a combined enum, `hypothesis_class` is coded `not_stated` rather than guessing `superiority` or `non_inferiority` by default; the raw `nonInferiorityType` string is also preserved alongside `hypothesis_class` in `analyses.csv`. For `analyses[]`, explicit within-single-arm tests (e.g. `Change within treatment group`) are excluded from between-arm analyses, whereas between-arm treatment-difference analyses where the submitter checked only one `groupId` in `groupIds` are retained with `groups_compared` recorded verbatim as posted (never imputing the unchecked comparator ID).
8. **Measure-level vs. analysis-level counts for `estimate_type_as_posted` and `hypothesis_class`**: Because a single HbA1c primary outcome measure can post zero, one, or multiple between-arm analyses, counts for `estimate_type_as_posted` and `hypothesis_class` are reported **both** across all posted between-arm analyses (denominator = all posted between-arm analyses) **and** across HbA1c primary measures (denominator = all posted HbA1c primary measures).
9. **`participants_started` and `baseline_tables.csv` scope**: None of the 248 ClinicalTrials.gov v2 records posts a `Total` group in `participantFlowModule`; every record posts per-arm `STARTED` counts in `participantFlowModule.periods[0]`. Both the verbatim per-group `STARTED` counts and their arithmetic sum across Period 1 flow groups are reported in `cohort_sheet.csv` without reconciling with `baseline_population_total`. `baseline_tables.csv` contains one row per baseline row label for `race`, `ethnicity`, `sex`, and `age` (including both continuous and categorical age measures where both are posted; when a single baseline measure combines race and ethnicity rows in one table, it is indexed under both `race` and `ethnicity`), while per-group baseline HbA1c values from `baselineCharacteristicsModule` are stored in `cohort_sheet.csv` (`baseline_hba1c_json`) and `readings.jsonl`.
